Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short

Packlog FPV does not collect, send or sell your data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Everything you save in the app stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

Packlog FPV is made and published by Mateusz Czarczyński (“we”). For anything about privacy, write to packlogfpv@mateuszczarczynski.pl.

What the app stores

The app stores only what you enter or scan:

  • packs: name, cell count, cell type, capacity, connector, voltages, purchase date and notes,
  • pack events: charges, flights, crashes, storage, IR measurements, damage and notes, with date and time,
  • settings: language, theme, reminder thresholds and the date of the last backup.

This data lives only in your phone’s storage. We don’t send it to any server and we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan QR codes on packs. Camera images are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • NFC: to read and write tags on packs. A tag holds only a link with the pack’s random identifier, no name or history. Any phone with NFC can read that link, but it reveals none of your data: that stays in your app.
  • Notifications: reminders about packs left charged or discharged too long are scheduled by the phone itself. They are local notifications, with no notification server involved.

You can revoke any permission in your phone’s settings. The app keeps working, just without that feature.

Backup

Only you create a backup, with “Export backup”. The app then writes a file with your packs and their history and opens the system share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example iCloud, Google Drive or a message. From then on, the file is subject to the rules of the service you chose. When restoring, the app reads only the file you pick.

In-app purchase

Packlog Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment entirely: we never receive your card or account details. The app remembers on your phone only whether Pro is active, and may check it with the store when you are online.

Internet

The app works offline. It connects to the internet only when you open this page or when the store verifies your Pro purchase.

Crash reports

If you allowed sharing diagnostics with app developers in your phone’s settings, Apple or Google may pass us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs. The app itself contains no reporting or analytics tools.

This page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy statement. The page uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts.

Your rights

Because we don’t process your data on any server, we have nothing to give you, correct or delete. You are in full control on your phone: you can edit and delete packs, export everything to a file or delete the app with all its data.

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed to handle your request.

If you believe we break data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example if the app were ever to send any data, we’ll mention it in the update notes and ask for your consent in the app before it takes effect.
